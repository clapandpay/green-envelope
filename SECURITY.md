# Security

Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting
instead: the **Security** tab of this repository, then **Report a vulnerability**. The
conversation stays private until a fix is out.

Green Envelope holds money in a link. The most serious reports are those that let someone
other than the holder of a link claim it, reveal a key, or build a spend that differs from what
the page shows. It has had internal review only, no external audit.

The wallet in the browser is for small amounts sent for fun, not for keeping money: see
[docs/wallet-security.md](docs/wallet-security.md) for what it protects against and what not.
